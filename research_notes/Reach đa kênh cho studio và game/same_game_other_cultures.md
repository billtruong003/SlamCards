# The same childhood card-flip game in other cultures: names, demand and landing-page angles

Scope: cultures that play a game like Vietnamese "đập hình / bạt dép ăn hình", where you flip a face-down card or tile by slamming, throwing or making a gust of air, and keep it if it flips. The goal is multilingual reach for "Dép Lào Tuổi Thơ / Flip-Flop Slam" (play.billthedevstudio.com/slam-cards/), which ships in vi, en, es, id, ja, ko, pt-BR, th, zh-Hans and zh-Hant.

Method note: about 22 web searches and fetches, run on 2026-10-22. Google Trends, YouTube and TikTok pages, and app-store analytics could not be queried directly from this environment. Every demand figure below therefore comes from news or analytics write-ups, and anything not in a cited source is marked as an inference or a gap.

## Korea: 딱지치기 (ddakji), the Squid Game effect and existing ddakji games

### Takeaway
Ddakji has by far the strongest global awareness of any version, because it is the opening game of Squid Game. Season 1 (2021) put it in the series' recruitment scene, season 2 (Dec 2024) made it the first stage of the pentathlon, and it returns at the end of season 3 (June 2025). The show set Netflix records each time. Demand is heavily tied to the show, though, and ddakji-specific search and app numbers were not findable. The 2021 clone wave proves the reach and also shows the trademark limits.

### Cited Findings
- Ddakji (딱지) is a folded-paper square, rectangle or circle used in *ddakji chigi* ("hitting ddakji"). It is played in both Koreas and dates to at least the Joseon period. Other names: ttakji, ttakchi, and dialect forms ttaegi, ttangji, ppachi, pyo — [Wikipedia: Ddakji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ddakji)
- Main rule (*neomgyeomeokgi*): the losers of rock-paper-scissors put their ddakji on the ground, and each player in turn throws theirs hard to flip an opponent's. Flipped ddakji are lost. Variants add boundaries or a set flipping order. *Bureomeokgi* moves ddakji by blowing air, which is the closest match to our gust mechanic — [Wikipedia: Ddakji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ddakji)
- Ready-made, cartoon-printed ddakji were popular in South Korea from the 1970s to the 1990s, which is the same "printed character card" format as Vietnamese hình — [Wikipedia: Ddakji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ddakji)
- Squid Game uses ddakji in season 1 episode 1, as the first stage of the season 2 pentathlon, and at the end of season 3. Some casinos began offering ddakji games after the show — [Wikipedia: Ddakji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ddakji)
- Gong Yoo's "salesman" recruits players by playing ddakji with them, and Netflix's season 2 teaser was built around his return — [GMA Network](https://gmanetwork.com/lifestyle/news/116379/gong-yoo-returns-as-recruiter-in-squid-game-2/story); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/showbiz/2024/10/01/the-salesman-is-back-get-ready-for-round-two-of-squid-game-netflix-teases-dec-26-return-with-gong-yoo-video/152268)
- Search effect in 2021: Squid Game was the No. 9 most-searched keyword worldwide on Google in 2021 (Google Korea) — [Daum/Yonhap](https://v.daum.net/v/20211209145923474). Google said searches for the show's games "red light green light" and "honeycomb cookies" hit record worldwide highs. Ddakji was not named in that release — [KTVU/Fox](https://www.ktvu.com/news/top-entertainment-related-searches-of-2021-include-squid-game-alec-baldwin). A Google Trends analysis said the show briefly passed searches for a celebrity, a president and "cats", and that "won to dollars" searches rose about 100-fold — [The Why Axis](https://thewhyaxis.substack.com/p/man-squid-game-is-freaking-huge)
- Social effect in 2021: TikTok said #Ddakji videos reached 17.6 million views within a few months — [NME](https://www.nme.com/news/tv/squid-game-leads-to-uk-origami-boom-3090196). A man in a suit playing ddakji with commuters at Pasar Seni MRT in Kuala Lumpur drew millions of TikTok views, one video over 2.7M — [SAYS](https://says.com/my/fun/videos-of-man-in-suit-recruiting-players-for-squid-game-at-mrt-pasar-seni-go-viral). For scale, #dalgona reportedly passed 1 billion views — [NRJ](https://www.nrj.fr/actus/squid-game-quelle-est-la-recette-du-dalgona-candy-devenue-virale-sur-tiktok-71355589)
- Later seasons: season 2 (released 26 Dec 2024) drew about 68M views in its first week, a Guinness record for most-viewed Netflix show in one week, and was No. 1 in 92 countries — [Advanced Television](https://advanced-television.com/2025/01/02/squid-game-season-2-sets-netflix-viewing-records); [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/775437-most-viewed-show-on-netflix-in-one-week). It passed 152.5M views by late January 2025, a figure from a single report — [We Are Resonate](https://www.weareresonate.com/2025/01/squid-game-season-2-breaks-netflix-viewing-record-with-68-million-views). Season 3 (27 June 2025) drew 60.1M views in three days — [Señal News](https://senalnews.com/en/data/netflixs-squid-game-third-season-scored-601-million-views-in-three-days); [Wikipedia: S3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squid_Game_season_3)
- Existing games: a "Squid Game Online" multiplayer browser game (Unity) is on CrazyGames with a 9.0 rating — [CrazyGames](https://www.crazygames.com/kr/game/squid-game-online). No ddakji-specific browser game or app with published download numbers turned up in search — [search results, see Gaps]

### Inferences
- Ddakji is the one name in this list that people outside its home country already know. A Korean page called "딱지치기" plus an English section aimed at "ddakji game online" is the highest-awareness angle. Interest probably spikes around Squid Game releases and decays between them (an inference from the 2021 clone decay below; Trends data was not checked).
- Our mechanic is closer to the *bureomeokgi* air-gust variant than to the main throw-to-flip rule. The page can honestly say "딱지치기, but with a slipper and a gust of wind."

## Japan: めんこ (menko)

### Takeaway
Menko is the historical ancestor of the whole family, including pogs via Hawaii. It is played by slamming your card near an opponent's so the impact flips it, which is very close to our mechanic. In Japan it reads as a Showa-era retro toy rather than a live trend, and 2024–2026 demand evidence was not found.

### Cited Findings
- The rule: a player slams a card on the ground near an opponent's cards and keeps any card that flips from the impact — [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/fun/toys/weird-history-pogs)
- Origin dates vary: the Edo period (1603–1867) in several articles, versus the mid-Meiji period (1880–1885) per Web Japan. Early pieces were clay, wood or ceramic, and cardboard versions later became forerunners of trading cards — [Web Japan](https://web-japan.org/kidsweb/virtual/fukuwarai/fukuwarai01.html); [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/fun/toys/weird-history-pogs)
- Before the late-1970s anime boom, menko cards printed with TV and manga characters were children's closest thing to an anime game — [Our Star Blazers](https://www.ourstarblazers.com/vault/328)
- Japanese immigrants took the game to Hawaii, where it became milk caps and then POGs — [Wikipedia: Milk caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_caps_(game)); [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/fun/toys/weird-history-pogs)
- Traditional toys of this kind are still sold in toy shops and 100-yen stores — [Stripes Japan](https://japan.stripes.com/travel/japanese-toys-childhood.html)

### Inferences
- The best page title is "めんこ" (hiragana, the common modern spelling). It can be paired with "昭和レトロ" (Showa retro) and "懐かしい遊び" (nostalgic games), which are standard Japanese nostalgia hooks (inference, not keyword-verified).
- Menko has no trademark issue. It is a generic folk-game name.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 Japanese search or social data on menko was found. No menko mobile or browser game with download numbers was found.

## Brazil and Portugal: "bater bafo" / "bafo" with figurinhas

### Takeaway
Bafo is the closest mechanical match after Vietnam: face-down stickers (figurinhas) on the ground, flipped by a cupped-hand slap whose air pushes them over, and you keep what flips. The 2026 World Cup sticker album (980 stickers, 48 teams, the largest ever) made figurinhas a national topic in 2026, with local bafo championships, so this market is the strongest timely, non-trademarked opportunity.

### Cited Findings
- Rules: two or more players put figurinhas face down and take turns slapping to flip them. The flipper keeps whatever turns face up. The usual technique is the cupped hand ("concha"), which moves air under the stickers. That is the same gust physics as our slipper — [Magazine Luiza blog](https://www.magazineluiza.com.br/blog-da-lu/c/li/lven/como-jogar-bafo-com-figurinhas-regras-e-dicas-para-ganhar/14226/)
- Folk tip: wetting the palm with saliva helps (a personal memoir, not a rule) — [Recanto das Letras](https://www.recantodasletras.com.br/causos/4478502)
- Live events: a free bafo championship in Aracaju followed "the traditional rules of bafo" — [Notícias do Brasil](https://noticias-do-brasil.news/brasil/cidades/campeonato-de-bafo-gratuito-em-aracaju-15-e-1607.html)
- 2026 album: 980 stickers (68 special), 112 pages, the largest collection ever because of 48 teams. Packs of 7 cost R$7 (Panini) — [Querobolsa](https://querobolsa.com.br/revista/quantas-figurinhas-tem-o-album-da-copa-de-2026); [SBT News](https://sbtnews.sbt.com.br/noticia/brasil/veja-detalhes-do-album-de-figurinhas-da-copa-do-mundo-de-2026). Experts estimate it can take more than 7,000 stickers to finish because of duplicates, which fuels trade meetups — [Folha Vitória](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/esportes/quase-mil-figurinhas-album-da-copa-2026-impulsiona-troca-de-repetidas-no-es/). Press framed it as a family tradition passed between generations — [Diário de Pernambuco](https://www.diariodepernambuco.com.br/esportes-dp/selecao-brasileira/2026/05/11713809-album-da-copa-2026-colecao-de-figurinhas-vira-paixao-compartilhada-em-familia.html)
- Existing digital versions: small indie titles only, such as "Bafo Tycoon" on itch.io and a game-jam entry. No traction data — [itch.io Bafo Tycoon](https://gurjista.itch.io/bafo-tycoon); [itch.io jam](https://itch.io/jam/pgjam3/rate/4930446)

### Inferences
- The pt-BR page title should be "Bater Bafo Online", with a subtitle like "o bafo da infância, com chinelo". "Bafo" is generic and has no trademark. Avoid "Panini" and "Copa do Mundo/FIFA" marks in titles. "figurinhas" is a safe, generic word.
- The World Cup itself ended in July 2026, so the peak may already be past (inference). Album and duplicate culture continues, and the next Brazilian league or Libertadores albums are recurring hooks.
- Portugal: the same word appears in some sources for Brazil only. I found no Portuguese (pt-PT) evidence.

### Gaps
- No Google Trends figures for "bater bafo" or "bafo figurinha" (Bill should check 2026 Brazil around May–July).
- The Portuguese Wikipedia article URL tried returned 404, and no pt-PT usage evidence was found.

## Philippines (teks), Indonesia and Malaysia (gambar/kartu), Thailand, China and Taiwan, Latin America and Spain (tazos), US (pogs)

### Takeaway
Teks (Philippines) is a close cousin: small, cheap, illustrated cards flicked or tossed, where the face-up card wins. It now has heritage-preservation coverage. Indonesia's "kartu umbul / gambaran" is a living, cheap card culture still sold on Tokopedia, but the slap-flip version was not well documented. Taiwan's 尪仔標 and China's 拍画片 are known nostalgic toys with thin online evidence. Tazos had a Mexican 30th-anniversary relaunch in 2024. Pogs are a mid-90s US fad whose name was a protected mark.

### Cited Findings
- Philippines, teks: players bet on which side lands up after flicking or tossing the cards, and the face-up card wins. The cards are about an inch tall and printed with pop culture from Dragon Ball Z to Darna. A pre-war edition with Filipino movie stars existed as early as the 1930s — [FilipiKnow](https://filipiknow.net/traditional-filipino-toys/); [TheSmartLocal PH](https://thesmartlocal.ph/filipino-childhood-games/); [Wikipedia: Milk caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_caps_(game)). It has been framed as intangible heritage worth preserving — [Traditional Sports & Games](https://traditionalsportsgames.org/news/teks-preserving-filipino-card-flip-games-as-intangible-heritage-and-cultural-education). Note that "teks" today more commonly means "to text" in Tagalog — [TagalogNow](https://tagalognow.com/dictionary/teks)
- Indonesia: kartu umbul is described as a traditional children's game (for example Lombok Tengah), with cards of numbers, flowers, fruit and cartoon characters — [Jurnal UNPAS](https://journal.unpas.ac.id/index.php/pendas/article/download/13848/6357/60884); [UAD eprint](https://eprints.uad.ac.id/52338/5/Karya%20cipta%20Permainan%20Kartu%20Umbul%20sebagi%20media%20edukasi%20gizi%20bagi%20siswa%20sekolah%20dasar_compressed.pdf). "Kartu tepuk" (slap cards) and umbul packs of 320–640 cards are still sold on Tokopedia — [Tokopedia](https://www.tokopedia.com/find/kartu-tepuk-gambar). An Android student project "Dhambul 3 in 1" digitised it — [Telkom University](https://repositori.telkomuniversity.ac.id/pustaka/files/127453/bab1/aplikasi-game-dhambul-3-in-1-berbasis-android.pdf)
- Malaysia: lists of 90s childhood games circulate on SAYS — [SAYS](https://says.com/my/seismik/benda-yang-hanya-kanak-kanak-90-an-saja-akan-tahu). Malaysians also reacted strongly to ddakji via the KL MRT video — [SAYS](https://says.com/my/fun/videos-of-man-in-suit-recruiting-players-for-squid-game-at-mrt-pasar-seni-go-viral)
- China: the family is listed as "wáah pín / huà piàn" (画片), with rectangular or circular printed cards — [Wikipedia: Milk caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_caps_(game)). Chinese nostalgia ("童年回忆杀") mini-games do exist, for example a capsule-toy collection game — [ifanr](https://www.ifanr.com/app/1041394)
- Taiwan: 尪仔標 is a staple of 古早童玩 (old-time toys) from about the 1950s–70s (民國40–60年代). Revived and licensed retro remakes have commercial value — [mybest TW](https://tw.my-best.com/115691); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/80393/post/200201030055/)
- Catalonia: patacó, played with cut-up Spanish playing cards — [Wikipedia: Milk caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_caps_(game))
- Mexico, tazos: Sabritas/PepsiCo marked 30 years of Tazos in June 2024 with a Mexican national-team edition of 20 Tazos plus digital "NFTazos" — [Vanguardia](https://vanguardia.com.mx/coahuila/te-acuerdas-de-los-tazos-la-moda-de-los-90-esta-de-regreso-LF12358131); [PepsiCo press PDF](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/f5a06aa7-7851-4041-8747-6cac3e35db8a/-/inline/no/VF_070624_Tazos%20est%C3%A1n%20de%20vuelta%20.pdf). The 2025 Sabritas "Las Favoritas de Ayer, Hoy y Siempre" retro campaign was about packaging and music, not Tazos — [Expansión](https://expansion.mx/mercadotecnia/2025/10/08/sabritas-trae-de-vuelta-los-noventa). Press in 2026 still runs Tazos nostalgia stories — [Tus Buenas Noticias](https://www.tusbuenasnoticias.com/noticias/economia/2026/02/23/57149-quien-invento-los-tazos-de-sabritas)
- US, pogs: they came from Hawaiian milk caps. A 1991 Oʻahu teacher (Blossom Galbiso) revived the game, which went worldwide by 1993. Canada Games went out of business in 1997, and many schools banned it as gambling — [Wikipedia: Milk caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milk_caps_(game)); [Mental Floss](https://www.mentalfloss.com/fun/toys/weird-history-pogs)

### Inferences
- Thailand: no Thai-language source on a slap-flip card game was found (search for ตบการ์ด returned nothing relevant). The th locale should probably lead with the Squid Game/ddakji link or a plain description such as "เกมตบให้พลิก" rather than claim a local tradition (inference).

## Best page title per language, and trademark risk

### Takeaway
Use generic folk-game names, which carry no trademark, as page titles: 딱지치기, めんこ, bater bafo, 尪仔標, 拍画片, teks, kartu umbul/tepuk. Never put "Squid Game / 오징어 게임", "POG(S)" or "Tazos" in titles, the app name, the logo or the art. Netflix filed SQUID GAME trademarks widely, won a domain case, and Apple rejects Squid Game copycats. POG was a held trademark, and Tazos is a Sabritas/PepsiCo brand.

### Cited Findings
- Netflix filed "SQUID GAME" trademarks in the Philippines in classes 9, 16, 18, 21, 25, 28 and 41 (class 9 covers software and class 41 covers games and entertainment), plus Taiwan (魷魚遊戲 and SQUID GAME, Oct 2021) — [Asia IP Law](https://asiaiplaw.com/jurisdiction/canada); [TechNews TW](https://technews.tw/2021/10/26/netflix-applied-for-squid-game-trademark-registration/)
- Netflix won a WIPO case over the domain Squid-Game.io, which used a look-alike logo — [CCTLD.ru](https://cctld.ru/en/media/news/industry/37138)
- Apple rejected a Squid Game companion app under Guideline 4.1 (Copycats) and asked for proof of rights to trademarked content — [Apple Developer Forums](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/693600)
- Netflix said in 2021 it was monitoring for copyright infringement and had no deals with clone makers — [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/15360/more-than-200-games-related-to-the-show-squid-game-raises-concerns)

- Tazos is a Sabritas/PepsiCo product line — [PepsiCo press PDF](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/f5a06aa7-7851-4041-8747-6cac3e35db8a/-/inline/no/VF_070624_Tazos%20est%C3%A1n%20de%20vuelta%20.pdf)

## Games that won reach by riding a pop-culture moment (Squid Game clones), and the limits

### Takeaway
In October 2021, Squid Game clones dominated mobile downloads. Three of them made the global top 10, with 53.2M combined installs (Sensor Tower), and one alone reached 36.3M (AppMagic). The wave fell 2.7x within weeks. Copycats using copyrighted art were pulled, and Apple rejects Squid Game copycats. The lesson: a moment can lift a generic-mechanic game, but only one that avoids the IP's name and art.

### Cited Findings
- Sensor Tower, October 2021: Candy Challenge 3D, Cookie Carver and 456 (Abi Game Studio) all reached the global top 10 by downloads, with 53.2M combined installs. Candy Challenge 3D was No. 2 with about 19M. The No. 1 game, Garena Free Fire, had about 34M — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/top-mobile-games-worldwide-october-2021-by-downloads); [Game Dev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-top-downloaded-mobile)
- AppMagic: "456: Survival Game" reached 36.3M downloads. ### Inferences
- The clones that worked used generic names for the mini-games ("Candy Challenge", "Cookie Carver", "456") rather than "Squid Game". The same pattern suits us: "ddakji" is a public-domain folk game, so we can name it freely. The show's logo, the green tracksuits, the pink guards, the salesman character and the shapes-on-card art must stay out.
- Ethical limits: avoid "official"-looking claims, Netflix-style art and misleading ad keywords. Bidding on "squid game" as a search-ad keyword is risky under platform trademark policies (unverified, needs a policy check).
- Timing: clone downloads fell 2.7x within about a month, so a moment-tied push should be built in advance of an event (new season, remake, World Cup album), not after it.
