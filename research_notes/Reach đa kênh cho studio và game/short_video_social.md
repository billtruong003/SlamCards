# Short video and social search reach for a free browser party game (Vietnam first, then SEA)

Scope note: about 16 searches and fetches. Most "how the algorithm works" material online comes from marketing blogs and SEO vendors, not the platforms. Each finding is labelled **[OFFICIAL]** (platform's own statement), **[PRACTITIONER]** (agency, creator or vendor claim) or **[CASE]** (a reported example). Numbers without a primary source are flagged.

## 1. TikTok: search and SEO, Vietnam behaviour, cadence, hooks, duets/stitches, Creator Rewards, Search Insights

### Takeaway
TikTok's official guidance is short: hook viewers in the first 3 seconds and post at a steady pace you can keep up. It also offers an official keyword tool, Creator Search Insights, that shows what people search for. The detailed "SEO weightings" (captions vs on-screen text vs speech) come only from practitioners. Creator Rewards is not open to Vietnam-based creators and needs videos over one minute long, so it does not matter for this studio. In Vietnam, TikTok has about 50M users and is the number-one app for people aged 26 and under. Facebook is still the default app for aimless scrolling.

### Cited Findings
**Search and SEO**
- [OFFICIAL] TikTok's Creator Search Insights is "a new tool, available in select regions" that shows creators the topics people search for on TikTok, so they can make content to match. Creators can long-press a topic to remove or report it. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights?lang=en)
- [PRACTITIONER] Access: search "Creator Search Insights" in the app. It is open to accounts with Creator Tools access. The "Searches by followers" filter needs at least 1,000 followers. Other filters include "Content gap". Third-party guides disagree on the filter names, and some list "Trending/For You" instead. — [Reacher](https://www.reacherapp.com/blog/creator-insights-tiktok-how-to-use-creator-search-insights-in-and-fix-it-when-its-not-showing); [360om](https://www.360om.agency/news-insights/tiktoks-creator-search-insights-how-to-level-up-your-content-with-trending-topics)
- [PRACTITIONER] TikTok reportedly transcribes speech and reads on-screen text for indexing. One vendor claims on-screen text carries weight "roughly equal" to spoken keywords. TikTok has not confirmed this. — [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/social-search-how-to-optimize-for-discoverability-on-tiktok-and-instagram-2025); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/tiktok-seo/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Put the main keyword in the first characters of the caption, because the start of the caption is said to count most. Keep auto-captions on and fix errors, because a mis-transcribed keyword drops out of the index. Use search autocomplete to find the phrases people actually type. Sources disagree on hashtags vs keywords. — [Keyweaver](https://keyweaver.io/blog/tiktok-seo-guide-captions-hashtags-covers); [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/tiktok-seo/); [SEO Sherpa](https://seosherpa.com/tiktok-seo/)
- [PRACTITIONER / survey, global] Adobe Express survey, as quoted by Vietnamese outlets: about 40–41% of respondents use TikTok as a search engine, including 64% of Gen Z and 49% of Millennials. 91% still rate Google as most useful. — [CMO Group Vietnam](https://cmo.com.vn/vuot-mat-google-tiktok-tro-thanh-cong-cu-tim-kiem-duoc-ua-chuong-boi-64-gen-z-va-49-millennials/); [DCCA](https://dcca.org.vn/en/51-gen-z-lua-chon-tiktok-de-tim-kiem-thay-vi-google)

**Vietnam-specific**
- [OFFICIAL, TikTok-reported] TikTok Vietnam says TikTok Shop GMV grew 66% in 2025, and revenue from search activity on the platform grew 55% in 2025. Not independently verified, and about commerce, not games. — [GenK](https://genk.vn/tiktok-shop-tang-truong-66-tai-viet-nam-khong-chi-luot-video-nguoi-dung-con-len-tiktok-de-tim-kiem-truoc-khi-mua-hang-165260729122737146.chn)
- [PRACTITIONER] One Vietnamese analysis says TikTok search data in Vietnam is still "slow and inaccurate" compared with markets like the US. It also says short video suits entertainment and news better than in-depth topics. — [MarketingAI](https://marketingai.vn/su-khac-biet-giua-hanh-vi-tim-kiem-tren-tiktok-va-google-194250416095030435.htm)
- [Research firm] Decision Lab (2024): Vietnam has "nearly 50 million TikTok users". — [Decision Lab](https://www.decisionlab.co/blog/the-fengshui-formula-behind-tiktoks-meteoric-rise-in-vietnam)
- [Research firm] Decision Lab Q1 2025: Facebook is still the top platform for aimless scrolling. TikTok, Zalo and Instagram grew the most. TikTok's favourability rose only among Gen Z. Q3 2025: platform loyalty is softening and engagement fell on the top entertainment platforms. — [Decision Lab Q1 2025](https://www.decisionlab.co/blog/connected-consumer-report-q12025); [Decision Lab Q3 2025](https://www.decisionlab.co/blog/vietnams-digital-landscape-q3-2025)
- [Research firm] Q&Me 2025: TikTok is the number-one app among people aged 26 and under. Its usage "reaches 20%" (the metric is unclear in the snippet). — [Q&Me](https://qandme.net/en/report/viet-nam-mobile-app-popularity-2025.html)

**Cadence and hooks**
- [OFFICIAL] Creator Academy: "your exact posting cadence is totally up to you". It recommends a steady stream of high-quality content over daily posting for its own sake. Hook viewers within the first 3 seconds: ask a question the video answers, show a timer, or tease the end result early. Make the first frame different or eye-catching. — [TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/8-tips-for-becoming-a-successful-TikTok-creator?lang=en)
- [PRACTITIONER] Third-party advice ranges from 1–4 posts a day to 2–3 a week. One claim that "63% of top-performing videos" hook in 3 seconds is credited to TikTok for Business but could not be verified. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/); [Teleprompter](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-3-second-rule)

**Creator Rewards**
- [OFFICIAL/PRACTITIONER] Eligibility (Creator Academy, plus guides that agree): 18 or older, 10,000+ followers, 100,000+ views in the last 30 days, a personal (non-business) account, videos over 1 minute, and pay only for "qualified views" (For You feed, 5+ seconds, unique accounts). Duets, stitches, photo posts and sponsored posts do not qualify. Eligible countries per Creator Academy: US, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, France, Mexico, Brazil. **Vietnam is not listed.** Some third-party lists add Italy and Spain. — [TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/article/creator-rewards-program); [Clip-short](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/tiktok-creator-rewards-program); [Quasa](https://quasa.io/media/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-eligible-countries-in-2026)
- "Search Highlights": no official TikTok feature by that name was found. The closest official tool is Creator Search Insights.

### Inferences
- Game-name search is near zero, so the TikTok keywords to target are the problem people are trying to solve, not the brand. Examples: "game chơi cùng bạn bè online", "game nhậu", "trò chơi tuổi thơ", "dép lào", "game không cần tải". Say the phrase out loud, put it on screen in the first frame, and put it at the start of the caption. Use Creator Search Insights and autocomplete to check which phrasing gets searched.
- Creator Rewards does not apply (Vietnam is not eligible, and the one-minute minimum does not fit short game clips). Don't plan around it.
- Facebook is still the scrolling default for older Vietnamese users, and TikTok for under-26s. The "tuổi thơ" (childhood nostalgia) angle probably lands with 8x/9x users on Facebook, and the gameplay and comedy angle with Gen Z on TikTok.

### Gaps
- No primary data on how much TikTok weights captions vs on-screen text vs speech. TikTok has not published it.
- No Vietnam-specific survey on TikTok as a search engine. The figures are global Adobe numbers.
- No official TikTok statement on duets and stitches as a growth driver for new accounts, and no data on trends and sounds for game clips. Not researched in depth within the call budget.
- Couldn't confirm whether Creator Search Insights is available in Vietnam. Bill should check in the app.

## 2. YouTube: Shorts discovery, long-form SEO, chapters, and Google video results / "Short videos" carousel in Vietnam

### Takeaway
Since 31 March 2025, YouTube counts a Shorts view as soon as playback starts, so raw Shorts views are inflated. "Engaged views" is the real measure. Shorts are shown first to a small test audience, then widened if it responds well. For Google, chapters set in the YouTube description are the official way to get "key moments". Google shows a short-video carousel drawing on YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, but no data exists on how often it appears in Vietnam.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, via secondary] YouTube changed Shorts view counting: announced 26 March 2025, in effect 31 March 2025. A view now counts when playback starts or replays, with no minimum watch time. The older metric remains as "engaged views" in Analytics, and monetisation still uses engaged views. — [Outlierkit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-algorithm-updates/); [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-does-youtube-shorts-algorithm-work)
- [OFFICIAL, 2023 Creator Insider, via secondary] The Shorts algorithm finds a small group of people who may enjoy a Short, shows it to them, and decides next steps based on their response. — [Outlierkit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- [PRACTITIONER, unconfirmed] Shorts older than about 28–30 days may be deprioritised (from a retention strategist's data, not confirmed by YouTube). A claim that first-frame view counting spread to all formats in August 2026 is also unconfirmed. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm-may-now-favor-fresh-over-evergreen/562661/); [Outlierkit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-algorithm-updates/)
- [PRACTITIONER] A 2025 analysis of 5M short videos found YouTube interactions fell by nearly 50% year on year, even as Shorts output rose. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/social-media-short-video-report-2025/)
- [OFFICIAL] Google Search detects video segments automatically, but "will prioritize key moments set by you, either through structured data or the YouTube description". For videos embedded on your own site, use Clip structured data (start/end time plus a label) or SeekToAction. — [Google Search Central, Video SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video); [Google Search Central, Video structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/video)
- [PRACTITIONER] Some guides say key moments need the video to be at least 30 seconds long. — [Swarmify](https://swarmify.com/blog/video-schema-markup/)
- [PRACTITIONER] Google's "Short videos" SERP feature pulls from YouTube and TikTok (Semrush), and earlier tests linked TikTok and Instagram Reels clips. — [Semrush KB](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1348-serp-features-short-videos); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/google-adds-short-form-video-carousel-in-selected-search-results/592729/)
- [PRACTITIONER, unverified] "62% of Google universal results include video, 26% a video carousel" (no named original study). — [SEO ScaleUp](https://seoscaleup.com/blog/video-seo-statistics-2026/)

### Inferences
- Judge YouTube Shorts by engaged views and subscriber conversion, not raw views. The 1.5k-view Short should be checked against its engaged views.
- Long-form is where Google video results can work. A "how to play" or "game nhậu cho nhóm bạn 4 người" video with description chapters can pick up long-tail Google and YouTube search. Embedding it on the game's landing page with VideoObject markup adds a second entry point.
- If the ~30-day freshness claim is true, a steady weekly Shorts cadence matters more than relying on a back catalogue.

### Gaps
- No data on how often Google shows the "Short videos" carousel in Vietnam, or for Vietnamese-language game queries. Semrush and Ahrefs SERP-feature datasets would be needed.
- No official YouTube source fetched directly. The Shorts facts are relayed by third parties.

## 3. Facebook Reels and groups (8x/9x nostalgia), Instagram Reels, Threads in Vietnam

### Takeaway
Facebook is still Vietnam's main scrolling platform and skews older (25–35+), which matches the 8x/9x "dép lào tuổi thơ" nostalgia angle. Reels are recommended to non-followers by interest. Threads is growing in Vietnam: roughly 4–4.65M monthly users (third-party estimates), and about 7% of Threads' global traffic, in the top 5 countries. Meta says it is growing "very well", especially among young people and office workers.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL, Meta Vietnam] Meta's Vietnam country director says Threads is growing very well in Vietnam, especially among young people and office workers, and expects it to be "even more promising in 2026". Meta does not publish per-country user numbers. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/dai-dien-meta-threads-tang-truong-rat-tot-tai-viet-nam-5010759.html)
- [PRACTITIONER estimates] Threads Vietnam is about 3.8–4M MAU (SimilarWeb plus Social Vietnam 2025), or about 4.65M users at end-2025 (Meta ad-tool reach). SimilarWeb (Sept 2025) puts Vietnam at about 7.33% of global Threads traffic, top 5. — [MKT](https://phanmemquangcaomkt.com/so-luong-nguoi-dung-threads-o-viet-nam/); [Raccoon](https://raccoon.vn/chi-tiet-tin-tuc/thong-ke-so-nguoi-viet-nam-su-dung-mang-xa-hoi-threads-hien-nay-352)
- [OFFICIAL, global] Threads passed 400M MAU in Aug 2025 and reached 500M later. Vietnamese press reports it passed X in daily active users in Sept 2025. — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/mang-xa-hoi-threads-can-moc-500-trieu-nguoi-dung-2526862.html)
- [Research firm] Facebook is still the top platform for aimless scrolling in Vietnam (Decision Lab Q1 2025). — [Decision Lab](https://www.decisionlab.co/blog/connected-consumer-report-q12025)
- [PRACTITIONER] Facebook Reels ranking uses the same AI system as the main feed. It predicts interest from past interactions and posts users hid or paused, and recommends to people with similar interests, not just followers. The Facebook audience skews older than TikTok's, which suits 25–35 targets. — [Revu Vietnam](https://blog.vn.revu.net/revu-thuat-toan-reels-facebook/); [Minh Duong Ads](https://minhduongads.com/bi-quyet-lam-reels-facebook-trieu-view/)
- [CASE] An example nostalgia community: the page "Hội những người thích hoài niệm" (8x retro games, anime, manga) has about 111K followers. — [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/Gamexua199x/)
- [PRACTITIONER, older] Indie guides say game videos get the best results when posted to Facebook groups. — [itch.io guide](https://itch.io/t/646271/promoting-your-game-with-zero-budget-the-big-indiedev-marketing-guide)

### Inferences
- Cross-post every TikTok clip as a Facebook Reel with a nostalgia caption ("Ai tuổi thơ từng chơi đập dép?" — roughly "Who used to play flip-flop slapping as a kid?"). Share selected clips into retro and nostalgia groups and pages, and follow each group's self-promo rules.
- Threads suits a text-plus-clip "building in public" voice aimed at young office workers (the "game cho team building / nhậu" crowd, i.e. games for team events and drinking nights). It is cheap to try because it shares Instagram identity.

### Gaps
- No 2025 Vietnam data on Reels reach, or on member counts of the largest 8x/9x nostalgia groups.
- Instagram Reels in Vietnam was not researched separately. Decision Lab only notes Instagram growing in Q1 2025.

## 4. Building in public and devlogs: what worked for solo devs, and case studies with numbers

### Takeaway
The recurring pattern in reported cases: post gameplay moments early and often, the first viral clip is one that makes clear what the game is, and creators who were never asked often bring more reach than the dev's own account or paid deals. For browser games, the best documented case (Globle) grew from one well-framed post in the right niche subreddit, then spread through organic sharing on Twitter.

### Cited Findings
- [CASE, founder interview] Globle (free browser game, solo dev Abe): posted to r/geography and the same post to a much larger web dev subreddit. The geography post took off and the web dev post got nothing. Peak was over 1M players a day during the Wordle craze. Hosted on Cloudflare's free static tier, almost no cost in year 1. Over 7 figures of revenue across about 17 games in about 3 years (ads via Nitro, plus an ad-free subscription). Paid creator deals brought players who did not stay. The best results came from creators he never contacted, for example a Thai TikToker playing his English-only game for a Thai audience, and a biology teacher who plays MetaZooa on TikTok daily. He also cross-promotes with Nerdle. — [Naavik podcast](https://naavik.co/podcast/the-solo-developer-making-seven-figures-from-free-browser-games/)
- [CASE] Tiny Glade (2 devs): its first viral TikTok, which made clear what the game was, is at about 9.7M views. Over 1M wishlists with zero paid marketing. — [OP Game Marketing](https://opgamemarketing.substack.com/p/tiny-glade-an-indie-game-by-2-devs)
- [CASE, secondary] Sledding Game (solo): posted clips for over a month before the store page went live. Best clip about 2.3M views on Instagram, about 15,800 wishlists on day one, over 100k copies sold in 5 days. — [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/indie-games-viral-tiktok)
- [CASE] YAPYAP: most impressions came from "showcase content creators" reposting the trailer, not the dev's own account. — [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/indie-games-viral-tiktok)
- [CASE] Senate Run (browser game) went viral, especially on TikTok, right after release, and went offline when traffic exceeded its free hosting. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senate_Run)
- [CASE] The Password Game (solo-built in 2 months): played over 10M times. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Password_Game)
- [CASE, 2022] A visual novel TikTok got over 571k views and over 2,900 wishlists over several days. — [How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/02/07/seven-great-tips-for-marketing-your-indie-game-on-tiktok/)
- [CASE, cautionary] A small multiplayer team reported paid TikTok ads performed worst for a PC game, and wishlists stayed at about 700. — [Medium post-mortem](https://medium.com/kolektif-gamedev/post-mortem-publishing-my-first-ambitious-game-as-a-solo-developer-kind-of-3c468e9270d2)
- [PRACTITIONER] Boosting your best organic posts can cost less per conversion than cold ads. Suggested pre-launch budget is about $200 a month (unverified). — [Gamosy](https://gamosy.com/blog/tiktok-game-marketing)
- [PRACTITIONER, older] #screenshotsaturday is reported anecdotally as the strongest tag, used with #indiedev, #gamedev and #indiedevhour. Lead with a good GIF. — [itch.io guide](https://itch.io/t/646271/promoting-your-game-with-zero-budget-the-big-indiedev-marketing-guide)

### Inferences
- The Reddit lesson from Globle is to post in the community whose interest the game matches (nostalgia, Vietnam, party games, r/WebGames), not in dev communities. In Vietnam, the equivalents are Facebook groups and VOZ-style forums.
- Make the room link playable instantly and robust to traffic spikes (the Senate Run outage is the warning).
- For a party game, make it easy for creators who were never contacted to play: a streamer mode, no sign-up, room codes readable on stream.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 solo-dev post-mortem with TikTok view-to-player conversion for a *web party* game was found.
- No current data on #screenshotsaturday performance on X, and none on X's reach for devlogs in 2025.

## 5. Creator and KOL/KOC seeding in Vietnam: costs, streamers, pitching

### Takeaway
Agency rate cards for Vietnamese micro creators range from about 0.6M to 10M VND per post (roughly US$25–400). They disagree with each other, and none cites a primary survey. Big gaming streamer groups (Độ Mixi / Refund Gaming) are now YouTube-only entertainment streamers and well beyond a zero-budget reach. No evidence was found of them playing small web party games.

### Cited Findings
- [PRACTITIONER, agency] Micro KOL (10k–50k followers): 5–10M VND per booking in one guide, 3–10M VND in another. — [Revu](https://blog.vn.revu.net/thue_kol/); [Chodat](https://chodat.com.vn/tin-tuc/bang-gia-booking-kol-koc-viet-nam-moi-nhat)
- [PRACTITIONER, agency] Micro-KOC (10k–100k): 1–2M VND (2024 guide). Another TikTok guide gives 1–3M VND. — [Upbase](https://www.upbase.vn/blog/bang-gia-booking-koc); [Revu](https://blog.vn.revu.net/booking-kol-tiktok/)
- [PRACTITIONER, agency] Micro review TikTokers: 612,000–3,100,000 VND (2026 listing). — [TCC Agency](https://tcc-agency.com/gia-thue-tiktoker-review/)
- [PRACTITIONER] One guide quotes 1–10M VND per 1,000 views. This looks implausible next to the per-post figures, so treat it as unreliable. — [Revu](https://blog.vn.revu.net/gia-thue-kol-tiktok/)
- [Background] Refund Gaming started as Độ Mixi's PUBG team (PGI 2018 Berlin) and became an entertainment streamer group. Độ Mixi won "Streamer of the Year" in 2023 and Most Popular Game Streamer at Nimo Global Gala 2024. He left NimoTV to stream only on YouTube. — [Wikipedia VI](https://vi.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%90%E1%BB%99_Mixi); [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/refund-gaming-tu-nhom-game-thu-choi-vi-dam-me-bat-ngo-giat-top-1-giai-quoc-te-va-tro-thanh-tuong-dai-streamer-lung-lay-trong-cong-dong-game-viet-20221110113728504.chn)
- [CASE, other market] Gartic Phone's popularity in Korea rose from July 2021 because many broadcasters played it. Having almost no player cap made it good for viewer-participation streams. — [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/Gartic%20Phone)
- [CASE] Globle's founder found paid creator deals brought low-retention players, and creators he never contacted brought the best results. — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/podcast/the-solo-developer-making-seven-figures-from-free-browser-games/)

### Inferences
- With a near-zero budget, the best return is likely gifting and pitching to small Vietnamese TikTok and YouTube gaming or comedy creators (5k–50k followers) who already do "play with viewers" content. Pitch: free, no install, 4 friends join via one link, a streamer room with viewers, and a funny "đập dép" moment in 30 seconds. One or two paid tests at about 1–3M VND each can measure what a creator post converts to (room creations from a tracked link).
- Viewer participation matters: a party game capped at 4 players limits viewer-join streams. A spectator or "chat votes" mode would make streamers more interested (an inference from Gartic Phone's no-cap appeal).

### Gaps
- No source confirms Refund Gaming or other Vietnamese streamers playing Gartic Phone, skribbl.io or similar browser party games. No rates found for mid-tier Vietnamese gaming streamers.
- No Vietnam-specific pitching best practice found from a reliable source.

## 6. Viral loops built into the product, and evidence from web party games

### Takeaway
Hard numbers are scarce. The documented evidence is qualitative: Gartic Phone and Among Us spread through streamers in 2020–2022, Globle through players sharing and tagging the dev, and Wordle-style daily sharing drove discovery. No data was found on how well share clips, link previews, challenge flows or referral codes convert in web party games.

### Cited Findings
- [CASE] Gartic Phone: Brazilian studio Onrizon, web release December 2020. It became very popular alongside Among Us in 2021–2022 through streamers, and its popularity has declined since. It runs in any browser on PC, tablet and phone, needs little teaching, and fits chat participation. — [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/Gartic%20Phone); [Streamerpedia](https://streams.fandom.com/wiki/Gartic_Phone); [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/guides/what-is-gartic-phone-and-why-do-streamers-love-it-the-multiplayer-drawing-game-explained)
- [PRACTITIONER] The pandemic made "everyone joins from their phone" party formats explode. Gartic Phone "more or less defined the genre in 2020". — [GameBuddies](https://gamebuddies.io/blog/free-browser-party-games-ecosystem-2026)
- [PRACTITIONER] Player caps: skribbl.io 2–12 (another source says 16), Gartic Phone 4–30. — [OnlineParty.games](https://onlineparty.games/compare/gartic-phone-vs-skribbl-io); [DoodleDuel](https://doodleduel.ai/compare)
- [CASE] Globle spread when players shared it and tagged the dev on Twitter. Daily habits and social sharing drove discovery (the share format is not described in detail). — [Naavik](https://naavik.co/podcast/the-solo-developer-making-seven-figures-from-free-browser-games/)
- [CASE, other market] Gartic Phone in Korea took off once broadcasters adopted it, from July 2021. — [NamuWiki](https://en.namu.wiki/w/Gartic%20Phone)

### Inferences
- The room link is already the loop: each game brings 1–3 new players. The cheap multipliers are:
  1. A rich preview card for the room link (og:title / og:image saying "Bill rủ bạn vào phòng đập dép", i.e. "Bill invites you to a flip-flop slapping room"), because Messenger, Facebook and Zalo show these cards in chats.
  2. A 5–10 second auto-generated result or highlight clip or image with the URL burned in, sized for TikTok and Reels.
  3. A rematch or "challenge another group" CTA at the end of a game.
- Referral codes are mostly a fit for paid economies. The game stays free (Bill's 22/10 decision), so referrals add little beyond share links.

### Gaps
- No measured data (k-factor, invite conversion) for skribbl.io, Gartic Phone, Jackbox or Among Us. Jackbox room-code and Among Us streamer-driven growth were not found in a citable source within the budget.
- Zalo's link-preview behaviour (which Open Graph tags it reads, cache rules) was not found in any official Zalo doc. It should be tested directly.

## 7. A realistic weekly content calendar for a one-person team

### Takeaway
No source gives a validated one-person calendar. The official TikTok guidance is a cadence you can sustain plus a strong first 3 seconds. Practitioners suggest anything from 2–3 posts a week to several a day. YouTube Shorts may favour fresh uploads. The calendar below is an inference that turns those findings into a 4–6 hour/week plan.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Sustainable cadence over daily volume, and a 3-second hook. — [TikTok Creator Academy](https://www.tiktok.com/creator-academy/en/article/8-tips-for-becoming-a-successful-TikTok-creator?lang=en)
- [PRACTITIONER] Ranges from 1–4 a day to 2–3 a week. — [Hootsuite](https://blog.hootsuite.com/tiktok-algorithm/); [Teleprompter](https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/tiktok-3-second-rule)
- [PRACTITIONER, unconfirmed] Shorts may lose distribution after about 28–30 days. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm-may-now-favor-fresh-over-evergreen/562661/)
- [CASE] Successful indies posted gameplay clips for weeks before and around launch (Sledding Game: over a month of clips). — [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/indie-games-viral-tiktok)

### Inferences (proposed calendar, not sourced as a best practice)
- **Batch day (Sun, about 2h):** record 4–5 clips from real play sessions or bot matches. Each clip opens with a payoff frame plus on-screen keyword text in Vietnamese, and the keyword is spoken aloud.
- **Mon/Wed/Fri:** TikTok post, then the same clip as a YouTube Short and a Facebook Reel within a few hours. The caption leads with a search phrase ("game chơi với bạn bè không cần tải…" — "a game to play with friends, no download needed…").
- **Tue:** share one Reel or clip into 1–2 nostalgia or party Facebook groups, following each group's rules.
- **Thu:** a Threads or X devlog post ("built-in-public" line plus GIF). On Saturday, a #screenshotsaturday post for the English and SEA audience.
- **Monthly:** one long-form YouTube video ("Hướng dẫn chơi" / funny full match, i.e. a how-to-play guide or a full funny match) with description chapters, embedded on the landing page with VideoObject markup. Pitch 3–5 micro creators.
- **Weekly review (30 min):** Creator Search Insights topics, engaged views on Shorts, and room creations from tracked links (UTM per platform).
