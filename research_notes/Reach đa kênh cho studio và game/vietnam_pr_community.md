# Vietnam PR, community buzz and partnerships for "Dép Lào Tuổi Thơ" (solo dev, free nostalgic browser party game)

Research date: 22/10/2026. About 20 search and fetch calls. Many forum rule pages and streamer collaboration processes were not reachable through search. They are listed under Gaps and not invented. "UNVERIFIED" marks claims from background knowledge or weak sources.

## 1. Vietnamese indie and nostalgia games that got reach (2018-2026), and how

### Takeaway
The clearest nostalgia cases from 2025-2026 are **Trở Về Tuổi Thơ (Corochti)**, **Tiệm Phở của Anh Hai** and **Goosing Out**. Their reach came from three things: Apple App Store featuring, streamers playing the game, and organic Vietnamese press that loves the "game Việt gợi nhớ tuổi thơ" angle. None of the press articles found was labelled sponsored. Flappy Bird is the only proven mega-viral case, and that was 2013-2014, outside the window. Several names in the brief could not be verified as Vietnamese games.

### Cited Findings
**Trở Về Tuổi Thơ, Corochti (closest comparable)**
- Corochti was founded in 2025 by Nguyễn Chấn Nam and Võ Nguyễn Minh Thông, with game designer Thanh Quyết. The game is a life sim of 1980s-90s village life, released just before Tết 2025. It ranked near the top of the App Store for two weeks. Apple rejected it five times before approving it. — [Genk](https://genk.vn/gap-go-nhung-studio-game-indie-viet-nam-dang-chinh-phuc-app-store-20250531181821936.chn)
- The Google Play listing shows 5M+ downloads, about 129K reviews and a 4.5 rating. The VN App Store shows 598 ratings at 4.6. The game is free with in-app purchases. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=vn.corochti.returntochildhood&hl=en_US), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tr%E1%BB%9F-v%E1%BB%81-tu%E1%BB%95i-th%C6%A1/id6740632670?l=vi) (figures as summarised by search; they vary by date and storefront)
- The follow-up game, Xóm Nhỏ Tuổi Thơ, was featured by Apple ("Games Come Home", World Animal Day). Kenh14 covered it on 11/10/2026 with no sponsored label, though the story leans on studio statements and studio images. Hàng Rong Tuổi Thơ is in progress. — [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/tu-xom-nho-viet-nam-den-khat-vong-dua-ban-sac-viet-vuon-tam-the-gioi-215261011191638361.chn), [Genk](https://genk.vn/thu-ngay-game-viet-xom-nho-tuoi-tho-va-loat-game-bao-ton-dong-vat-hoang-da-duoc-apple-gioi-thieu-nhan-ngay-dong-vat-the-gioi-165260310204017718.chn)
- What it shows: launching right before Tết, a "tuổi thơ" brand used as a series, and platform featuring all helped. No social-media numbers were published.

**Tiệm Phở của Anh Hai (free itch.io game, 2025)**
- Made by a student at Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội. Free on itch.io, in Vietnamese and English. The phrases "Phở Anh Hai" and "số 10 Đan Phượng" trended on Google Trends and in delivery apps, players went looking for the real place, and Vietnamese and foreign streamers livestreamed it. Kenh14 ran the story on 3/11/2025 as a reprint from Đời sống Pháp luật, with no sponsored label. No play counts were published. — [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/game-do-nguoi-viet-san-xuat-gay-bao-mang-dan-tinh-do-xo-tim-mot-dia-chi-215251103193535446.chn)
- What it shows: a free web or itch game can get national press when streamers pick it up and a meme forms around a concrete, findable detail.

**Goosing Out (2025)**
- Made solo by freelance painter Hồng Hải Đăng, who did the concept, pixel art and code (he learned to code from YouTube) in about two weeks. Tuổi Trẻ Online ran a feature on 21/11/2025, framed as "trở về tuổi thơ bị ngỗng đuổi". The article does not say how the game got noticed and gives no numbers. — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/game-goosing-out-dua-nguoi-choi-tro-ve-tuoi-tho-bi-ngong-duoi-20251121125453961.htm)
- What it shows: the "solo creator + childhood memory" story is itself the pitch that Tuổi Trẻ picked up.

**Flappy Bird, Nguyễn Hà Đông (2013-2014, outside the window but the reference story)**
- Released in 2013, it went viral in early 2014. It had 50M+ downloads on the App Store and earned about $50,000/day in ads (Đông to The Verge). Đông pulled it in February 2014, saying it "ruined my simple life". A blogger alleged bot downloads but could not prove it. — [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/sanfrancisco/news/creator-of-wildly-popular-flappy-bird-game-withdraws-app-from-online-stores), [MCV](https://www.mcvuk.com/business/flappy-bird-is-generating-50k-in-ad-revenue-every-day), [Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/world/flappy-birds-app-creator-pulls-popular-game-saying-it-ruined-his-simple-life)
- A PewDiePie link to its virality was **not confirmed** by the sources found.

**Other studios Genk cites as App Store success**
- ONDI Games (Hanoi, 2022): Fortias Saga was featured at Apple Indie Fest 2024. Imba Games: Kawaii Home Design, 30M users. Both credit App Store featuring as the main lever. — [Genk](https://genk.vn/gap-go-nhung-studio-game-indie-viet-nam-dang-chinh-phuc-app-store-20250531181821936.chn)

**7554 (Emobi Games)**
- An offline Windows FPS about Điện Biên Phủ (2011), described as the first modern-3D Vietnamese PC game. Emobi's history-game project got Tuổi Trẻ coverage and a WSJ mention in 2012. No sales figures were found. Outside the 2018-2026 window. — [Giant Bomb](https://giantbomb.com/wiki/Companies/Emobigames), [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/emobi.html)

- **Thời Loạn**: no game with this exact title and a developer was found. Near matches are Loạn Tam Quốc (GAMOTA, 2021) and Nam Đế: Cờ Lau Dựng Nước. — [FPT Shop](https://fptshop.com.vn/tin-tuc/giai-tri/loan-tam-quoc-175922), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/vn/app/nam-%C4%91%E1%BA%BF-c%E1%BB%9D-lau-d%E1%BB%B1ng-n%C6%B0%E1%BB%9Bc/id1159840538)
- **Tên Lửa** and **Chinh Đồ**: not searched separately for lack of budget. Unverified.

**Squid Game-style trends in Vietnam**
- VnExpress reported real-life "Squid Game" contests ("Thi 'Squid Game' ngoài đời"). — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/thi-squid-game-ngoai-doi-4370190.html)
- In 2021 the Play Store filled up with "con mực" clone games. — [Mytour](https://mytour.vn/vi/blog/bai-viet/dong-game-con-muc-tran-ngap-tren-play-store.html)
- In HCMC, Jackfruit Adventure sells "Saigon Survival Games" as indoor teambuilding: 8-250 people, 2-3 hours, with red light green light, honeycomb and hopscotch. — [Jackfruit Adventure](https://jackfruitadventure.com/tour/saigon-survival-showdown/)

**Evergreen nostalgia content**
- Kenh14 regularly runs listicles on childhood games ("thú vui thời chưa có game online", 9X games, browser "4 nút" games). These are hooks a pitch can match. — [Kenh14 2021](https://kenh14.vn/nhin-lai-nhung-thu-vui-thoi-chua-co-game-online-cua-gioi-tre-viet-ca-bau-troi-tuoi-tho-nhu-ua-ve-20210924094802776.chn), [Kenh14 2022](https://kenh14.vn/nhung-tua-game-huyen-thoai-gan-lien-voi-tuoi-tho-cua-game-thu-viet-nhin-thoi-ma-ky-uc-ua-ve-20220826170153297.chn), [Kenh14 browser games](https://kenh14.vn/thu-thuat/tro-ve-tuoi-tho-voi-game-4-nut-ngay-tren-trinh-duyet-201352819281397.chn)

### Inferences
- Dép Lào Tuổi Thơ fits the "game Việt + tuổi thơ + solo dev" frame that Kenh14, Genk and Tuổi Trẻ covered organically in 2025-2026. The Corochti, Tiệm Phở and Goosing Out stories can be cited in a pitch ("the next in this line").
- Because the game is browser-based there is no App Store featuring lever. Streamer pickup (the Tiệm Phở route) and press are the main substitutes.
- A concrete, shareable, real-world detail (like "số 10 Đan Phượng") helps memes form. A nostalgic card set, school-yard rules or a "which card was yours" prompt could play that role. This is speculative.

### Gaps
- No published traffic or play numbers for Tiệm Phở or Goosing Out. No Corochti social metrics or revenue.
- Bad Seeds, Thời Loạn, Tên Lửa and Chinh Đồ were not verified as Vietnamese indie games. Treat them as possibly misremembered.
- No case of a Vietnamese Facebook viral web or mini game with numbers was found in this pass.

## 2. Press: who covers indie and nostalgia, how to pitch, paid vs organic

### Takeaway
Kenh14 (Game/Mobile section), Genk, Tuổi Trẻ and VnExpress have all run organic, unlabelled stories on Vietnamese indie nostalgia games in 2025-2026. Paid PR ("bài PR / booking báo") is a large, openly sold market at roughly 2-65M VND per article through agencies. Paid links must carry rel="sponsored" for Google.

### Cited Findings
**Organic coverage examples (no sponsored label)**
- Kenh14 on Tiệm Phở of 3/11/2025. — [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/game-do-nguoi-viet-san-xuat-gay-bao-mang-dan-tinh-do-xo-tim-mot-dia-chi-215251103193535446.chn)
- Kenh14 on Corochti of 11/10/2026. — [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/tu-xom-nho-viet-nam-den-khat-vong-dua-ban-sac-viet-vuon-tam-the-gioi-215261011191638361.chn)
- Kenh14's profile of a young developer. — [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/tro-chuyen-cung-nha-phat-trien-tre-dua-game-viet-vuon-ra-the-gioi-215250531111859169.chn)
- Genk on App Store indie studios, which was based on a Thanh Niên Việt interview. — [Genk](https://genk.vn/gap-go-nhung-studio-game-indie-viet-nam-dang-chinh-phuc-app-store-20250531181821936.chn)
- Tuổi Trẻ on Goosing Out. — [Tuổi Trẻ](https://tuoitre.vn/game-goosing-out-dua-nguoi-choi-tro-ve-tuoi-tho-bi-ngong-duoi-20251121125453961.htm)

**VnExpress as an entry point**
- VnExpress runs Vietnam GameVerse and Vietnam Game Awards. The contact for these is gameverse@vnexpress.net, plus HuongTTT28@fpt.com, hotline 1900 633 003 (branch 3) and 084 7300 8899 ext. 8574. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/vietnam-game-awards-2026-la-gi-thoi-gian-de-cu-hang-muc-va-lo-trinh-cham-giai-5011562.html)

**Paid PR price ranges (agency and reseller pages, not official rate cards)**
- Kenh14: 4-65M VND per article depending on placement (MIC Creative). A "Bài loại 1 – Nhóm 1 (PC)" is about 18M and a special homepage article about 27M (Medialabs). A Top-1 PC homepage for 2 hours is about 60M, and Top-1 + Mix about 90M (agencyvn). — [MIC Creative](https://miccreative.vn/bao-gia-dang-bai-pr-tren-kenh14/), [Medialabs](https://medialabs.asia/our-update/digital-advertising/bang-bao-gia-quang-cao-tren-kenh-14-moi-nhat-304), [agencyvn](https://agencyvn.com/bao-gia-bai-pr-tren-kenh14), [webmedia](https://webmedia.com.vn/kenh14-vn/)
- VnExpress: the "Doanh nghiệp viết" section is about 7.8-15.3M VND. Ngôi Sao ranges from 5M (section) to 50M (homepage top). — [DigicomVN](https://digicomvn.com/book-bao-vnexpress/), [MIC Creative](https://miccreative.vn/bao-gia-bai-pr-vnexpress/)
- Genk: one reseller lists 2-3M VND, which is unusually low and unverified. — [HapoDigital](https://hapodigital.com/bang-gia-dang-bai-pr-tren-bao-genk-vn/)

**Google rules on paid links**
- Google's guidance reads: "Mark links that are advertisements or paid placements (commonly called paid links) with the sponsored value." It recommends "ugc" for comments and forum posts, and "nofollow" when the other values don't apply. — [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links)
- Google has penalised advertorials with PageRank-passing links (the 2013 Interflora case and UK newspapers). — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/after-penalizing-interflora-uk-newspapers-google-warns-against-advertorials-149371)

### Inferences
- For a free game with no budget, organic pitching is realistic: the comparable stories ran without a sponsored label. A pitch could be an email with the press-kit link, a 30-60s gameplay clip, a "solo dev from HCMC" angle and a hook to the season or nostalgia.
- Paid PR at 4-18M VND per article is a poor fit for a free, ad-funded game. If ever bought, the link to play.billthedevstudio.com should carry rel="sponsored". Vietnamese PR articles often do not, and that is a risk for the domain where AdSense is pending.
- Smaller game sites (GameK, Game4V, Gamelandvn, Mọt Game, 2game) are plausible targets for free web-game news.

### Gaps
- No public tip-line or editorial email was found for Kenh14 Game, Genk, GameK, Game4V, Gamelandvn, Mọt Game, 2game, Znews, Thanh Niên or VnExpress Số hóa. Look for "Liên hệ tòa soạn" in each site's footer.
- No source on whether GameK, Game4V and the other smaller game sites charge for indie news or how.

## 3. Communities and self-promotion rules

### Takeaway
No rule pages for Voz, Tinhte, Spiderum or the Vietnamese gamedev Facebook groups were retrieved. This is the biggest gap in these notes. The one adjacent public channel confirmed is the Game Jam Vietnam Facebook page.

### Cited Findings
- The Game Jam Vietnam Facebook page lists a Hanoi address and posts Global Game Jam Vietnam results. — [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/p/Game-Jam-Vietnam-100088721568141/)
- Searches for Tinhte, Spiderum and Voz advertising rules returned only Vietnamese anti-spam law. Thông tư 22/2021/TT-BTTTT applies to advertising messages, not forums. — [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/hoi-dap-phap-luat/quy-dinh-ve-gui-thu-dien-tu-tin-nhan-quang-cao-64406.html)

### Inferences (UNVERIFIED, from general knowledge, check before acting)
- Voz has dedicated sub-forums for advertising and buy/sell. Posting a link-only thread in general boxes is likely to be deleted. A "tự làm game tuổi thơ, xin góp ý" dev-diary post is the usual accepted format.
- Tinhte allows user posts but bans thin promotional posts. A behind-the-scenes "how I built it" story fits better.
- Spiderum accepts long-form essays. A personal essay on bạt dép, childhood and why the game was made fits its style, while hard selling does not.
- Reddit r/VietNam and Discord servers usually limit self-promotion to designated threads or channels.
- 8x/9x nostalgia Facebook groups are usually admin-approved, and many ban links. A short gameplay video or a memory prompt with the link in a comment is the common workaround. Ask the admin first.

### Gaps
- The exact current rules for each forum and group, and the names and sizes of the Vietnamese gamedev Facebook groups (Vietnam Game Developers, Indie Game Việt Nam), could not be retrieved by search.

## 4. Events and awards

### Takeaway
Vietnam Game Awards has an "Outstanding Indie Game" category (individual or team under 20 people). The 2027 cycle will probably open nominations around late January 2027, just after a Tết launch, but the 2027 dates are not announced. Garena's Vietnam Game Jam (HCMC, July) and Global Game Jam Vietnam (January) are the local jams. The Taipei Game Show Indie Game Award 2027 deadline (19/8/2026) has passed, and only the INDIE HOUSE waitlist remains.

### Cited Findings
**Vietnam Game Awards 2026 (part of Vietnam GameVerse 2026, theme "Do Local – Go Global")**
- Four groups. The Golden Sun group covers Vietnamese-developed games and includes the indie category.
- Eligible games had to be licensed and released in Vietnam between 25/2/2025 and 29/1/2026.
- Nominations ran 29/1-25/2/2026. The preliminary round (10-31/3) was 50% readers and 50% experts. The final round (7-21/4) was 70% judges and 30% community.
- Organised under the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism by the Department of Broadcasting, Television and Electronic Information and the Vietnam Game Alliance, with VnExpress hosting.
- Source: [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/vietnam-game-awards-2026-la-gi-thoi-gian-de-cu-hang-muc-va-lo-trinh-cham-giai-5011562.html)
- The 2025 edition had 22 awards and more than 3M votes. — [VnExpress](https://vnexpress.net/cong-nghe/vgv-2025/game-awards), [Kenh14](https://kenh14.vn/diem-danh-loat-giai-thuong-tai-vietnam-game-awards-2025-toan-cai-ten-khien-game-thu-gat-gu-215250528140136562.chn)
- The 2026 preliminary vote drew more than 33,000 votes. — [game.gov.vn](https://game.gov.vn/tin-tuc/hon-33000-luot-binh-chon-so-loai-vietnam-game-awards-2skr)
- "Licensed" is a hurdle. A free, ad-funded browser game may not count as licensed under Vietnam's game licensing rules (Nghị định 72/2013 and later decrees). This is UNVERIFIED and needs to be asked at gameverse@vnexpress.net.

**Garena Vietnam Game Jam 2026**
- The top 20 teams from an online selection went to a 48-hour offline round on 3-5/7/2026 at Hoa Lư Stadium, Đa Kao, HCMC, with travel support for out-of-town teams.
- Judging weights: gameplay 35%, theme 20%, art and audio 20%, technical 15%, presentation 10%.
- Sources: [gamejam.garena.vn](https://gamejam.garena.vn/en), [itch.io](https://itch.io/jam/vietnam-game-jam-2026)

**Global Game Jam Vietnam 2026**
- Returned in 2026 after almost a decade of editions in Vietnam. Registration closed 29/1/2026, and the jam runs 36 hours. — [VTV](https://vtv.vn/373-luot-tai-game-toan-cau-den-tu-studio-viet-100260115142130945.htm)
- The same VTV piece says 37.3% of global game downloads come from Vietnamese studios. — [VTV](https://vtv.vn/373-luot-tai-game-toan-cau-den-tu-studio-viet-100260115142130945.htm)

**Taipei Game Show Indie Game Award 2027**
- Free entry, with submissions through 19/8/2026. There were 731 entries from 53 countries, and 45 teams went to round two.
- The show runs 21-24/1/2027 at Nangang Hall 1. INDIE HOUSE exhibitor registration moved to a waitlist on 21/10/2026.
- Sources: [TGS](https://tgs.tca.org.tw/indie_award_e.php), [Events for Gamers](https://www.eventsforgamers.com/indie-game-award-2027-finalists-announced-731-entries-from-53-countries-and-regions-compete-for-top-honors/), [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/23340/taipei-game-show-2027-confirmed-for-next-january-global-exhibitor-recruitment-begins)

### Inferences
- Since TGS 2027 is closed, the realistic near-term moves are: Vietnam Game Awards 2027 nominations (if a browser game qualifies), GGJ Vietnam January 2027 for networking, and TGS 2028 (submissions likely mid-2027).

### Gaps
- No details found for Game Prime Thailand, Indie Arena Booth, BitSummit, HCMC university game clubs or the Vietnam Game Awards 2027 timeline.
- No Vietnam GameVerse 2026 venue or dates.

## 5. Schools, offices and youth organisations

### Takeaway
The only evidence found is that the Squid Game wave made "childhood folk games" a sellable teambuilding format in HCMC. No source was found on teachers using browser games in class or on partnerships with English centres or Đoàn Thanh niên.

### Cited Findings
- Jackfruit Adventure sells "Saigon Survival Games": 8-250 participants, 2-3 hours, Vietnamese-style childhood challenges and a "betrayer" role. — [Jackfruit Adventure](https://jackfruitadventure.com/tour/saigon-survival-showdown/)
- A Vietnamese student association abroad ran a Vietnamese "Squid Game" with Rồng Rắn Lên Mây. — [UNL](https://news.unl.edu/node/290926/)

### Inferences (speculative)
- A 2-4 player browser game suits a classroom break or a remote office icebreaker. It could be pitched to teambuilding companies as a digital warm-up.
- Risks with schools:
  - Ads shown to minors, which matters for AdSense family policies.
  - School Wi-Fi blocks.
  - A slap or strike mechanic could be read as violent.
  - Data collection from children.
- An ad-free "lớp học" mode would reduce these risks.

### Gaps
- No search-volume data for "trò chơi tuổi thơ cho học sinh".
- No case of an English centre or Youth Union partnering with a game.

## 6. Streamers and KOLs

### Takeaway
Độ Mixi (MixiGaming) is Vietnam's biggest gaming streamer: about 49K average viewers in 2025, second globally on YouTube Gaming. Streams Charts noted indie games as a standout of his 2025 content. Refund Gaming is his group (Mixi, Nhism, Rambo, Snake, QNT), now mostly entertainment streaming. Micro-KOL and KOC prices are cheap compared with press, roughly 0.6-10M VND per post, but these figures come from agency estimates.

### Cited Findings
**Độ Mixi**
- About 49K average viewers in 2025, second worldwide behind IShowSpeed per Streams Charts. Indie games and GTA V roleplay were highlighted, with 8.2M+ subscribers. — [24h](https://www.24h.com.vn/cong-nghe-thong-tin/do-mixi-bat-ngo-lot-top-2-youtube-gaming-streamer-toan-cau-nam-2025-c55a1728096.html)

- Small-dev outreach without money:
  - Email or Facebook-page message to the streamer's business contact with a 20s clip and a ready room link.
  - Pitch mid-size streamers who do "game lạ / game Việt" segments, since big names often find games through viewer suggestions.
- A paid micro-KOC test at about 1-3M VND per TikTok fits a donate/ads budget.
- Any paid post needs an ad disclosure.

## 7. Seasonal hooks

### Takeaway
